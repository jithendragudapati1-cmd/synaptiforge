YOUR DATA

Privacy notice

Synaptiforge is a small beta for learning from numeric CSV data and making predictions.

Information we store

We store your name, email address, a password hash if you use a password, and your Google account identifier if you use Google sign-in. We also store account verification and recovery records, email-delivery status, training uploads, saved models, checkpoints, job names, and model scores.

Prediction CSV files are processed to produce your download. The app does not intentionally save those prediction uploads or downloads as training jobs.

How we use it

Your information lets you sign in, receive account emails, train and resume models, manage your saved jobs, and make predictions. Your uploads are used to train the models you request. The app does not share your workspace with other users or use it to train a shared model.

Who can access it

The service operator can access stored information when running the service, investigating problems, handling support, or restoring backups. The hosting provider stores the app's data and may process technical request logs. Google processes Google sign-in and sends account emails when Gmail is configured. Signing in does not give Synaptiforge access to your Gmail inbox or Google Drive.

Cookies and technical records

We use an essential session cookie to keep you signed in and protect forms. The app has no advertising or analytics integration. Temporary abuse-prevention records include request IP addresses and expire from the app's rate-limit table after 15 minutes. Hosting logs follow the provider's retention settings.

Storage and deletion

Your saved jobs, uploads, models, and checkpoints remain until you delete them. Deleting a job removes its live files. You can download your data or permanently delete your account from Account settings. Deleting the account revokes its sessions and removes its live workspace.

Automatic backup snapshots are encrypted and kept for up to {{ retention }} days, with fewer snapshots when storage is limited. Deleted information may remain in those retained backups until they expire. Restoring a backup may temporarily restore old records; the restore tool reapplies the available deletion registry; the operator must review any other deletion requests before restoring service.

Account verification and recovery links expire automatically. Their audit records and email-delivery status may remain until account cleanup. Successfully sent verification/reset email content is cleared from the email queue.

Your choices

Use sample or anonymized data. Upload only data you have permission to use. You can change your display name, delete saved jobs, download your account data, delete your account, or contact the operator for help with these requests.

GET STARTED

Help & support

Train a model from examples, then use it to predict answers for new CSV rows.

Three steps

  1. Train: upload a CSV with numeric input columns and one answer column containing two possible values. Choose that answer column and start training.
  2. My Models: wait for completion, then select a saved model. Rename jobs or delete ones you no longer need.
  3. Predict: download the selected model's template, add your new input rows, upload it, and download predictions.

What the beta supports

Numeric inputs and binary classification: predicting one of two outcomes. Text inputs, multiple-outcome prediction, and predicting continuous numbers are not supported yet. Parallel training runs on one server.

If something goes wrong

Check that the CSV has unique column names, no blank values, and the same numeric input columns as your model's template. Verify your email before using the workspace. If training is queued, another job may be using the worker. A cancelled or paused job can resume when it has a saved checkpoint.

Predictions can be wrong. Confidence describes the model's estimate for that row; it is not a guarantee of correctness. Check held-out test scores before relying on a model.
